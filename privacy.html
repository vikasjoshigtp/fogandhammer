Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 1st april 2026   |   Effective: United States

Fog & Hammer ("Fog & Hammer," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website, fogandhammer.com, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Fog & Hammer provides design and production services in the United States, including signage programs, brand identity, pre-media production, packaging design, 3D rendering, print advertising, and related creative production services.

By using this website or submitting information through the contact form, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information you choose to provide. When you contact us through the website enquiry form or by email, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The content of your message or project enquiry

We do not collect sensitive personal information, payment card data, government-issued identification, or regulated data through the website.

If you email us directly at hello@fogandhammer.com, we may retain the information included in your message and any related communication.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information submitted through the website only for purposes directly related to your communication with us, including:

  • Responding to your enquiry or message
  • Communicating about your potential project needs
  • Understanding your design, signage, branding, packaging, pre-media, print, or production requirements
  • Providing follow-up communication related to your enquiry
  • Maintaining internal business records related to communications
  • Improving how we manage and respond to website enquiries

We do not use enquiry information for marketing purposes unrelated to your communication with us, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Contact Form and Enquiries

Our website includes a contact or enquiry form through which visitors may contact Fog & Hammer about potential services.

By submitting an enquiry, you understand and agree that:

  • Submitting an enquiry does not create a client relationship, confidentiality agreement, service engagement, or professional obligation unless separately agreed in writing.
  • You should not submit confidential business information, trade secrets, proprietary data, payment information, or regulated data through the website form. Fog & Hammer does not accept unsolicited confidential disclosures through the general contact form.
  • Any information you include in a general enquiry is received without any obligation of confidentiality on our part, unless we have separately agreed otherwise in writing.

4. Cookies and Analytics

This website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to support website functionality and to understand how visitors use our site. Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website.

The types of cookies this website may use include:

  • Strictly necessary cookies — required for the website to function correctly, such as maintaining session state and enabling the contact form. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — used to collect aggregated, anonymous information about how visitors interact with the website, such as pages visited and time spent on the site. This information helps us understand how the website is used and improve its content and performance. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics cookies.

This website does not use advertising cookies, remarketing pixels, or behavioral tracking technologies for commercial targeting purposes.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the website. For guidance on managing cookies in your browser, refer to your browser help documentation.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information submitted through the website.

We may share information only where reasonably necessary for standard business operations, including:

  • Responding to and managing your enquiry
  • Operating our website through our hosting provider
  • Processing contact form submissions through our email delivery systems
  • Supporting website analytics to understand visitor behavior in aggregate (described in Section 4)
  • Complying with applicable law, legal process, or lawful requests from government authorities
  • Protecting the rights, property, or safety of Fog & Hammer, our clients, or others

Any third-party service providers who handle information on our behalf are required to protect that information and use it only for the purposes for which it was shared. We do not authorize them to use or disclose your information for their own marketing or other purposes.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information submitted through the website from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

These measures include using secure email communications, maintaining appropriate access controls, and selecting reputable service providers for website hosting and email delivery.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. You should avoid submitting sensitive, confidential, or regulated information through the general website contact form.

7. Data Retention

We retain enquiry and contact information for the duration of the business relationship with the relevant individual or company, and for a reasonable period thereafter as required for legitimate business, legal, accounting, or operational purposes.

Where an enquiry does not lead to an active engagement, we retain the communication only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the enquiry and to maintain an accurate record of our correspondence.

Project-related communications, files, and records are retained for the duration of the client relationship and for such further period as may be required by applicable law or sound business practice.

After the applicable retention period, information is securely deleted or anonymized.
